# EthicalVPN press kit

Public product information for a newsletter, software directory or community resource mention. Prepared 8 November 2026 for release 0.2.0. This repository is maintained by the product publisher.

## One sentence

EthicalVPN is a free desktop VPN for Windows, macOS and Linux, funded through required, consent-based proxy sharing.

## Short description

Choose Germany, the United States, France or the United Kingdom without a VPN account or subscription. Free access requires in-app consent for third-party traffic to use your bandwidth, device resources and public IP. Sharing can continue after the app window closes. Withdrawing consent stops sharing and free VPN access. Read the exchange before installing.

## Ready-to-use community post

I work on EthicalVPN, a free desktop VPN for Windows, macOS and Linux. We would like feedback on installation and the clarity of our sharing disclosure.

The exchange is required proxy sharing: with your consent, third-party traffic can use your bandwidth, device resources and public IP. Sharing can run after you close the app window; withdrawing consent stops sharing and free VPN access. There are no cash payouts.

There are four locations: Germany, the United States, France and the United Kingdom. Current Windows packages are unsigned; macOS packages are ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized. Please review the installation instructions and funding model before deciding whether it suits you.

## Claims to preserve

The app provides a system-wide VPN connection. It does not provide a separate HTTP/SOCKS endpoint or independent exit IP for each browser profile. Torrenting is prohibited. There are no cash earnings. Do not describe this as audited, anonymous, open-source, Microsoft-certified or Apple-notarized. The public repository contains download documentation; application and SDK source are private. Checksums establish a match to published files, not a security audit.
