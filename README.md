## What makes it free?

Free VPN access requires explicit consent to proxy sharing. Third-party traffic can use your bandwidth, device resources and public IP. The sharing service can continue in the background after the app window closes. Withdrawing consent stops sharing and free VPN access. There are no cash earnings or payouts.

Please read [how sharing works](https://ethicalvpn.app/how-it-works/) before installing. The website's [privacy information](https://ethicalvpn.app/privacy/) and [terms](https://ethicalvpn.app/terms/) describe the service.

## Start here

1. [Choose your operating system and download the installer](https://ethicalvpn.app/downloads/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=free_desktop_20261108&utm_content=quick_start).
2. Read the installation steps shown on the download page. On Linux, choose the package for your distribution and CPU architecture.
3. Open EthicalVPN and review the sharing disclosure before consenting. Choose a location and connect.
4. To stop the exchange, withdraw sharing consent in the app. Closing the window alone does not stop background sharing.
