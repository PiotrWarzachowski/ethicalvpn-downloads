## What makes it free?

Free VPN access requires explicit consent to proxy sharing. Third-party traffic can use your bandwidth, device resources and public IP. The sharing service can continue in the background after the app window closes. Withdrawing consent stops sharing and free VPN access. There are no cash earnings or payouts.

Please read [how sharing works](https://ethicalvpn.app/how-it-works/) before installing. The website's [privacy information](https://ethicalvpn.app/privacy/) and [terms](https://ethicalvpn.app/terms/) describe the service.

## Feedback

Use [Issues](https://github.com/PiotrWarzachowski/ethicalvpn-downloads/issues) to report a reproducible problem. Include your operating system, CPU architecture, app version, steps and the exact visible error. Do not post passwords, private keys, full VPN configurations or personal information.

Known issue being investigated: on some macOS networks, background sharing can remain in a retrying state while the VPN is connected. Please include that status when reporting a connection or sharing problem.
